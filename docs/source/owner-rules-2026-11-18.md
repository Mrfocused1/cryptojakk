Complete Proposed Gameplay Rulebook \| For Paul Bridges \| 18 November 2026

STATUS: Draft for approval. Core gameplay is established, but several special-card rules below are proposed from the Street Blackjack UK variant and have not yet been approved as official Crypto Jakk rules. Confirm all choices before implementation.

# 1. Objective and game setup

**1.** Crypto Jakk is a shedding game, not casino 21.

**2.** The first player to get rid of all their cards wins.

**3.** Proposed game size is 2–6 players.

**4.** Each player starts with seven cards.

**5.** Use a standard 52-card deck with hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades.

**6.** Remaining cards form the face-down draw pile.

**7.** One card begins the face-up discard pile.

**8.** First player is seated left of the dealer.

**9.** Play initially proceeds clockwise.

**10.** Dealer rotates clockwise after each hand.

**11.** Players can compete against AI or humans online.

**12.** Opponents’ hands remain hidden.

# 2. Ordinary card-playing rules

**13.** Play a card matching the suit of the top discard.

**14.** Alternatively play a card matching its rank.

**15.** An Ace is wild except during an active pickup penalty.

**16.** Place a valid card on top of the discard pile.

**17.** If no legal move is available, draw one card and end the turn.

**18.** Unrelated ordinary cards cannot be played.

**19.** Multiple cards can be played when the full sequence is legal.

**20.** The last card determines the active top card and resolved special effects.

# 3. The 2 — pickup 2

**21.** A 2 creates a two-card pickup penalty.

**22.** Next player must respond with an eligible defence or draw the accumulated total.

**23.** Each further 2 adds two cards.

**24.** Two 2s mean pickup four.

**25.** Three 2s mean pickup six.

**26.** Taking the penalty ends that player’s turn.

**27.** A Red Jack can cancel a 2-based penalty if the relevant house rule is enabled.

# 4. Black Jacks — pickup 5

**28.** Jack of spades and Jack of clubs are Black Jacks.

**29.** A Black Jack adds five to the pickup penalty.

**30.** Two Black Jacks create pickup ten.

**31.** Further Black Jacks each add five.

**32.** A Red Jack can cancel the accumulated penalty.

**33.** A 2 may stack on a Black Jack only if mixed stacking is enabled.

**34.** A Black Jack may stack on a 2 only if mixed stacking is enabled.

# 5. Red Jacks — cancellation

**35.** Jack of hearts and Jack of diamonds are Red Jacks.

**36.** A Red Jack cancels an eligible active pickup penalty.

**37.** The pickup total becomes zero.

**38.** It can cancel eligible pickup effects earlier in the same turn.

**39.** A Red Jack does not create a pickup penalty.

**40.** Cancellation of a 2-based penalty depends on the mixed-stacking setting.

# 6. The 8 — skip (proposed; requires confirmation)

**41.** An 8 skips the next player.

**42.** Two 8s skip two player positions.

**43.** Three 8s skip three positions.

**44.** With multiple players, skips may cycle around the table.

**45.** In two-player games, an 8 returns the turn to its player.

**46.** That player must make another legal move or draw as normal.

# 7. King — reverse (proposed; requires confirmation)

**47.** A King reverses direction.

**48.** Clockwise becomes anticlockwise.

**49.** A second King reverses direction again.

**50.** A third King reverses it again.

**51.** In two-player games, any number of Kings returns the turn to the player who played them.

**52.** Show the direction change visibly.

# 8. Ace — suit change

**53.** An Ace can begin a turn regardless of suit/rank unless a pickup penalty blocks it.

**54.** An Ace can be included in a valid multi-card sequence.

**55.** If the turn ends on an Ace, its player chooses the active suit.

**56.** Available suits are hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades.

**57.** The chosen suit governs the next matching requirement.

**58.** Show the selected suit to everyone.

# 9. Queen — cover required (proposed)

**59.** A Queen must be covered by another card in the same turn.

**60.** Strict covering allows the same suit or another Queen.

**61.** Optional relaxed house rule allows any covering card.

**62.** Another Queen can cover a Queen regardless of suit.

**63.** An uncovered Queen causes a one-card penalty.

**64.** A Queen cannot be the final winning card.

**65.** This restriction applies even with a valid JAKK declaration.

# 10. Multiple-card play and runs

**66.** A player may play multiple cards in one turn.

**67.** Same-rank cards may be combined if the sequence is legal.

**68.** Ascending or descending same-suit runs are permitted under this proposed ruleset.

**69.** Each card must legally connect to the preceding card.

**70.** Runs can include special cards.

**71.** Special effects resolve over the full run.

**72.** Pickup effects can remain pending while a run continues.

**73.** The final result depends on the cards and order played.

**74.** A Red Jack can cancel eligible penalties earlier in the run.

**75.** Player explicitly ends the turn after completing the sequence.

**76.** Final card and resolved effects determine the next player’s state.

**77.** Example: 2♥, 3♥, 4♥, 5♥ is a proposed ascending run; the initial pickup effect does not remain when the run ends on 5♥.

# 11. Pickup penalties and stacking

**78.** Show the pickup total throughout.

**79.** A Black Jack adds five.

**80.** A 2 adds two.

**81.** Eligible cards can add to a running penalty.

**82.** Red Jacks cancel eligible penalties.

**83.** Next player defends or accepts the penalty.

**84.** Accepting means drawing the full total.

**85.** Drawing the penalty ends the turn.

**86.** Unrelated cards cannot bypass a pickup penalty.

**87.** Pending pickup effects from a multi-card run resolve according to the final sequence.

**88.** Show both pickup amount and its cause.

# 12. JAKK declaration

**89.** Players declare JAKK before the turn on which they intend to play their final card.

**90.** The JAKK button must be visible.

**91.** Declaration applies to one upcoming turn.

**92.** A player may declare while holding multiple cards.

**93.** If they do not finish on their next turn, the declaration expires.

**94.** A fresh declaration is required before a later attempt.

**95.** Finishing without valid declaration incurs one card.

**96.** Already-played cards remain discarded.

**97.** Penalised player stays in the game.

**98.** Explain the penalty onscreen.

**99.** Apply equally to human, AI and multiplayer participants.

# 13. Winning and ending a hand

**100.** A player wins by legally emptying their hand.

**101.** The JAKK declaration must be valid.

**102.** An uncovered Queen cannot win.

**103.** With power-card finishes disabled, players cannot finish on Ace, 2, 8, Jack or King.

**104.** With that option enabled, eligible power cards may finish.

**105.** In two-player mode, King or 8 cannot be the winning last card under the proposed Street Blackjack variant.

**106.** If attempted, the card is accepted but the player draws one and continues.

**107.** With Bring Backs off, a legal finish immediately ends participation.

**108.** With Bring Backs on, pending pickup penalties may return a finished player before play passes their position.

**109.** Confirm winner before ending the hand.

**110.** Show the winner.

**111.** Offer next hand or exit.

# 14. Starting cards

**112.** Host decides whether opening discard powers activate.

**113.** With First Card Active off, opening card is neutral.

**114.** With it on, opening 2 creates pickup two.

**115.** Opening Black Jack creates pickup five.

**116.** Opening Ace requires a suit choice.

**117.** Opening King reverses direction.

**118.** Opening 8 skips a player.

**119.** Opening Queen must be covered.

**120.** Opening Red Jack has no special effect.

**121.** Resolve opening effects before the first normal turn ends.

# 15. House rules

**122.** Show house rules before a match.

**123.** All players must know which settings are active.

**124.** Do not change settings mid-hand.

**125.** Changes require confirmation before a new game.

**126.** AI follows the selected settings.

**127.** Server enforces settings rather than trusting browsers.

**128.** Configurable options: flexible Queen covering, mixed 2/Jack stacking, Bring Backs, finish on power cards, and First Card Active.

# 16. Running out of cards

**129.** Monitor the draw pile.

**130.** When reshuffling is required, recycle eligible discarded cards.

**131.** Keep the top discard visible.

**132.** Shuffle remaining discarded cards into a new draw pile.

**133.** Define a deck-exhaustion procedure for insufficient cards.

**134.** Proposed variant: player with most cards sits out when deck is exhausted or a round passes without legal play.

**135.** Return that player’s cards to available cards.

**136.** If tied for most cards, end the hand without winner.

**137.** Players sitting out can rejoin next hand.

# 17. Invalid moves and penalties

**138.** Unmatched ordinary cards are invalid unless a special rule permits them.

**139.** Invalid moves do not alter official state.

**140.** Explain rejection.

**141.** Optional proposed variant: attempted invalid play costs two cards plus active pickup penalty.

**142.** Apply any enabled penalty consistently.

**143.** Do not allow out-of-turn actions.

**144.** Players can play only cards in their hands.

**145.** Reject duplicate submissions.

**146.** Reconnecting cannot bypass validation.

**147.** Timeouts must use the approved policy.

# 18. Multiplayer rules

**148.** Minimum two players per match.

**149.** Proposed maximum six.

**150.** Host creates a room and unique code.

**151.** Players join using code or invitation link.

**152.** Lobby shows participants.

**153.** Host starts the match.

**154.** Late joiners wait for next hand unless another policy is approved.

**155.** Hands remain private.

**156.** All players see identical discard, active suit, turn order and effects.

**157.** Only active player can act.

**158.** Handle disconnects.

**159.** Reconnect into active hand while room exists.

**160.** Rematch preserves agreed room settings.

**161.** Match may span several hands to a chosen win target.

**162.** First to reach target wins the overall match.

# 19. Turn timers

**163.** Host may enable or disable timers.

**164.** Proposed options: 20, 30, 45, 60 seconds.

**165.** Timer begins at start of active turn.

**166.** Show timer to active player.

**167.** Timeout invokes agreed automatic action.

**168.** Automatic action must be legal.

**169.** Apply consistently to human players.

**170.** Reconnection must not unfairly reset timer.

# 20. AI gameplay

**171.** AI follows human rules.

**172.** AI cannot see opponents’ hidden cards.

**173.** AI makes legal moves only.

**174.** AI understands pickup penalties and defences.

**175.** AI understands multi-card runs.

**176.** AI uses special cards appropriately.

**177.** AI follows JAKK requirements.

**178.** AI recognises legal wins.

**179.** Difficulty comes from strategy, not hidden information.

**180.** AI actions should be paced naturally.
